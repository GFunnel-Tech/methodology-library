# ED-02 — The Inbound System (ad → qualified booked call → client)

`source: stated` (S-02, S-03). He describes it as the installation program clients
use; it is the core of the Marketing + Sales pillars.

## Purpose
A repeatable, paid, inbound acquisition funnel that puts qualified prospects on a sales
call and improves its own targeting over time.

## Inputs
An offer worth a high-ticket price (ED-P01), an ad account, a CRM/calendar, an AI-driven
form and an email + SMS sender.

## Steps (his order, S-02)
1. **Ad** — short-video paid social. Creative is drawn from **9 ad types**: UGC/selfie,
   static text, green screen, carousel, podcast, professional, sketchbook, client win,
   testimonial.
2. **AI conditional-logic form** — qualifies the prospect as they answer; kept inside the
   ad platform's app so volume stays high and cost per lead stays low.
   - *Decision:* qualified? **No** → disqualify, route to a **resource path** (free
     content, stay in touch). **Yes** → step 3.
3. **Calendar** — prospect picks a call time.
   - *Decision:* booked? **Not yet** → **AI follow-up loop** (below). **Yes** → step 4.
4. **Thank-you page** — personal video, FAQs, confirmation checklist; its job is
   pre-selling and lifting the show rate.
5. **Closing call** — diagnose → present the offer → answer every question → close on
   the call.
6. **New client** — onboarding starts.

**Side loops**
- **AI follow-up loop:** every qualified lead who has not booked gets 7–10 days (D0–D10)
  of AI-written email + SMS that answers replies and always links to the calendar.
  Booking exits the loop immediately.
- **Signal loop:** qualified-lead data is sent back to the ad platform (conversion
  signal), so the platform finds more people like them the longer it runs.
