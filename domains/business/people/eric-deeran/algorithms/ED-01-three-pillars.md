# ED-01 — The 3 Pillars (Marketing · Sales · Fulfillment)

`source: stated` (S-01) unless marked.

## Purpose
His top-level model of a high-ticket service business: growth stalls when any one of
three functions is weak, and compounds when all three are run as systems.

## When to use
First diagnostic on any client: which pillar is the constraint right now?

## The three pillars and their components (S-01)

| Pillar | Job (his words, paraphrased) | Components he lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Marketing | fill the pipeline with high-intent leads | content systems · paid acquisition · positioning & offers |
| 2. Sales | close five-figure retainers on one call without pressure | discovery-call scripts · retainer pricing · objection handling |
| 3. Fulfillment | deliver consistently without the founder being the bottleneck | team building · client delivery · SOPs & systems |

## Steps — `source: derived` (from S-05 teardowns; to confirm against S-08/S-10)
1. Find the weakest pillar. In his teardowns the usual starting state is "word of mouth
   only, feast or famine": no marketing system, so pillar 1 is the constraint.
2. Fix the offer and pricing before driving traffic (positioning sits *inside* Marketing,
   but in several teardowns the repricing alone closed deals with no ads — S-05 Tori, Harp, Owl House).
3. Install acquisition (ED-02 Inbound System).
4. Install the sales process (closing call, proposal in options — ED-P01).
5. Systemize delivery and hire so the founder leaves the call and the work (S-05 Algonquin, Giard).
