# ED-07 — Booking form, triage call, show rate and the pillar pitch

`source: stated` (S-13, section "Discovery Call Workflow + Sales Meeting & Closing Deals" and
"Setting Up Your Call Calendar") unless marked. The full closing call itself is in videos only.

## Purpose
Qualify before the long call, get the prospect to show up prepared, and pitch by tying your
own steps to the pains they named.

## When to use
Every inbound or outbound lead that wants to talk.

## Inputs
A booking page in the CRM, a calendar, the "problems you solve" list, your 4–5 pillars.

## Steps (his order)
1. **Booking-form questions** (S-13 · "Qualifying Questions…"): name and phone; confirm they
   are the owner and will bring every decision-maker; type of business and what sets it
   apart; top priorities this year; biggest current challenges; average monthly revenue;
   past spend on video and why; anything else. No automatic cut-off on this form.
2. **Triage call** (S-13 · "How To Book Triage Calls"), a short call before the full meeting:
   1. what made them reach out;
   2. who they are — established or just starting; **never had a client or customer →
      disqualify**;
   3. their biggest goal, in numbers;
   4. where they are now against it (clients, typical price, monthly revenue);
   5. what they have tried (nothing → find out why and take doing it alone off the table;
      something → the biggest challenge);
   6. timing — if not now, when and why then;
   7. can they give about 1–3 hours a week `[tag: assumption] stated by S-13`;
   8. book the full video call; get a firm commitment to attend (he generally does not
      offer reschedules); confirm ~45 minutes, at a computer, somewhere quiet;
   9. partners or co-decision-makers must attend;
   10. if money has not come up, state the package range and ask whether it would block a fit.
   Tone: natural, low-pressure wording; do not over-qualify good leads.
3. **Protect the show rate** (S-13 · "How To Fix No Show Rates"), checked in this order:
   book only 48–72 hours ahead; keep enough open slots; cancel bad fits; do not overload them
   with material; automate reminders; call self-booked leads first to triage; review setter
   recordings for pushed bookings; send a short personal video; at the call time, call twice
   in a row; move no-shows to a CRM stage that starts a no-show sequence; double-book only as
   a last resort.
4. **Pitch with pillars** (S-13 · "4-5 Pillar Exercise" and "Problems You Solve"): list the
   4–5 stages you take a client through; in the pitch, link each stage to a pain they named
   earlier, then to the outcome, then ask a check-in question.
5. **Close and track** (S-13 · sales dashboard): outcomes recorded as first-call close,
   follow-up close, deposit, or down-sell; revenue sold vs. collected; referrals asked.
   `source: derived` — the tracked outcomes imply the close order; the script is not in the documents.

## Decision points
- No prior customers → disqualify. Partners not on the call → re-book with them.
- Money is a blocker → before the full call, not on it.
