# ED-13 — Finance controls: P&L ratios and the payroll cycle

`source: stated` (S-13, section "7 Figure Operations").

## Purpose
A monthly profit-and-loss view with target ratios, and a fixed pay cycle.

## When to use
Monthly, once there is a team to pay.

## Steps
1. **Fill the P&L** (S-13 · "How To Use A P&L & Template"): income lines — revenue contracted,
   cash collected, refunds, chargebacks, financed revenue, total income; expenses in three
   buckets — **acquisition** (ad spend, referral payouts, paid shout-outs, recruiting ads),
   **labour** (sales, fulfilment, marketing), **other** (bank fees, software, insurance,
   office, travel, training…); then net income and tax to set aside. Each line also as % of income.
2. **Check the ratios** against his targets (below).
3. **Run payroll twice a month** (S-13 · "How To Track Payroll + Payroll Periods"): process on
   the 10th for the 25th–9th, pay on the 15th; process on the 25th for the 10th–24th, pay at
   month end; one row per person split into wages and contractor pay (base, commission,
   bonus); an approval step before payment, then a paid flag.

## His stated targets (`[tag: assumption] stated by S-13`; falsifier: our own books)
| Ratio | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Cash collected ÷ revenue contracted | 60–70%+ |
| Acquisition ÷ income | 30–35% |
| Labour ÷ income | 25–30% |
| Other ÷ income | 5–10% |
| Net margin | 30–50% |
| Tax set aside | ~30% of net (his sample) |

## Decision points
- A bucket above its range → that cost is the first to cut. `source: derived`

## Sources
S-13 (P&L template, payroll template). The template has broken formulas in places; ratios
read from his stated targets, not his sample cells.
