# ED-08 — Ad and VSL structure, opt-in friction, and weekly funnel KPIs

`source: stated` (S-13, sections "Creating Your VSL & Ads" and "Setting Up Ads") unless marked.
Script *structure* only; no lines copied.

## Purpose
The creative and measurement layer under ED-02: what the ad and the video sales letter (VSL)
say, in what order, how to raise lead quality, and which numbers to check each week.

## When to use
Building or fixing the paid funnel.

## Inputs
The positioning line and offer (ED-05), 4–5 named pillars (ED-07), past client results,
the ad account and CRM.

## Steps
1. **Choose the ad type** (S-13 · "2 Types Of Video Ad Scripts"):
   - **Offer-forward** (≤ 30–60 s) — use when you have strong results and a guarantee.
     Order: hook calling out the buyer → their pain and the outcome they want → the offer and
     guarantee → why it works (past results) → watch the short video.
     Fewer, more problem-aware leads, each costing more.
   - **Indirect** (≤ 60 s) — a free system or training in exchange for contact details.
     Order: hook as a question → three pains and what they cost → "others felt the same" →
     the free system → an honest why (we earn your trust; you can also do it yourself) →
     where to send it. More, cheaper leads; more freebie-seekers.
   Lengths `[tag: assumption] stated by S-13`.
2. **Write the VSL** (S-13 · "VSL Full Example + Script"), sections in order:
   opening (who is speaking) → direct call-out of the audience and the outcome → erase doubts
   (feel–felt–found) → reframe a common belief (people buy from who they know and trust, not
   the best product) → reveal the named steps of your system → social proof → one or two
   sentences per step → the offer, the guarantee, and a call to book a fit call.
3. **Add friction to the opt-in, one question at a time** (S-13 · "Lead Quality Not Great?"),
   least to most sales-like: owner of a business in the niche? → ready to invest in content
   that brings customers? → how soon? → monthly revenue band. Disqualifiers: not the owner;
   not ready to invest; "a couple of months"; revenue under about $20k a month — the revenue
   line moves with your price `[tag: assumption] stated by S-13`.
   Add the next question only after watching lead volume.
4. **Track weekly** (S-13 · "Fill Out Data Tracking Sheet") — four weekly tabs, three blocks:
   advertising → funnel → sales. Look at the month too when the sales cycle is long.

## Decision points
- Strong proof and guarantee → offer-forward; need volume or cheaper leads → indirect.
- Lead quality poor → add the next friction question; lead flow collapses → remove it.

## Outputs
An ad, a VSL, an opt-in form and a weekly scorecard.
