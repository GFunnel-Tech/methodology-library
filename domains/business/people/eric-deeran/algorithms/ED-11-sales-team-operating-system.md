# ED-11 — Sales team operating system: hire, pay, run the day, forecast

`source: stated` (S-13, section "Hiring/Managing/Leading Your Team" and the sales dashboard)
unless marked.

## Purpose
Take the founder off the sales calls: hire setters and closers, pay them on results, run
them with two short daily meetings, and forecast the calls needed to hit a target.

## When to use
Once the funnel books more calls than the founder can take (ED-P01 move 9).

## Inputs
A working funnel (ED-02/ED-08), the sales dashboard, the CRM, a team chat.

## Steps
1. **Source** (S-13 · "How To Hire An Appointment Setter/Closer") — join high-ticket sales
   groups on Facebook; post separate ads for setters and closers; shortlist on experience
   and culture fit.
2. **Interview in two rounds and score** (S-13 · "Interview SOP: … Setter/Closer"):
   - round 1, two interviewers splitting topics: fit with company and role (full-time? side
     businesses?), experience and low points, sales history (product, price point, volume,
     close rate, income, process, lead sources), goals;
   - round 2: review a DISC profile with them, values and fit questions, their questions;
   - scorecard: 8 criteria × 1–5 per interviewer — drive, skill against the role's KPIs,
     coachability, honesty, clear direction, natural rapport, a believable reason for
     leaving, income goals that match the role (low goals count against). No pass mark stated.
   - red flags: cannot say why this company; no past failures; blames former bosses;
     unexplained job changes; neither market nor sales experience; ego; money as the only motive.
3. **Pay on results** — commission on cash collected: setter **5%** on closed calls they
   booked, closer **10%** `[tag: assumption] stated by S-13`; no base pay mentioned; paid as
   contractors.
4. **Run the day**:
   - **Start-of-day meeting**, weekdays (he holds it at 9am Eastern): wins → each person shows
     their tracking sheet and pipeline → today's book by role (closers: calls on the
     calendar, confirmed, projected closes; setters: bookings possible given closers' open
     slots; account managers: upsells and renewals) → gap to the company goal on a weekly
     tracker and the plan to close it → week's pipeline → blockers → call-review training
     (Tue–Thu) → close.
   - **End-of-day meeting, ~30 min**: stats against the morning commitment → tomorrow's
     appointments and confirmations → CRM hygiene (notes, task dates, new leads contacted,
     follow-ups done) → the weekly goal → close.
   - **1:1s**: the team member submits priorities and asks ahead; the manager picks one of
     three question sets (personal and productivity; engagement; alignment and culture).
5. **Track and forecast** (S-13 · sales dashboard): each rep logs daily outreach, calls
   offered and set, connected calls, offers, no-shows, closes by type (first call, follow-up,
   deposit, down-sell), revenue sold and collected, lead source, upsells, referrals.
   Forecast: **closes needed = target ÷ average sale; connected calls = closes ÷ close rate;
   scheduled calls = connected ÷ show rate.**

## His stated closer minimums (`[tag: assumption] stated by S-13`)
Close rate 30% of connected calls; cash collected 40% (of revenue sold, `source: derived`
reading); show rate 70%; one referral per new client on average. Conflicts with ED-07/ED-08
figures — H-12.

## Decision points
- A rep below minimum for the week → training via call review (stated); what happens after
  repeated misses is not stated (H-16).

## Outputs
A staffed, measured sales team and a weekly forecast.

## Sources
S-13 (hiring guide, interview SOP and scorecard, start-of-day, end-of-day and 1:1 templates,
sales dashboard).

## Open edges

- Commission-only pay may fall under employment rules depending on the country and state;
