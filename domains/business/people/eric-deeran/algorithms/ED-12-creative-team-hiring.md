# ED-12 — Creative team hiring: editors, videographers, producers

`source: stated` (S-13, section "Hiring/Managing/Leading Your Team").

## Purpose
Move delivery off the founder with contract editors, videographers and a producer, without
losing margin.

## When to use
When fulfillment, not sales, is the constraint (ED-01).

## Inputs
A portfolio of finished work to set the standard, a contractor agreement, a training doc.

## Steps (his order)
1. **Source** — he calls it "the Facebook method": join editor communities on Facebook, post
   a job, compare submissions on quality for the price. He prefers editors in lower-cost
   countries to protect margin (S-13 · "How To Hire A Video Editor").
2. **Interview in stages** (one SOP per role, same shape): screen the portfolio → round 1 on
   basics → round 2 on depth → evaluation (portfolio again in light of the answers, team fit)
   → written offer (pay, scope, start) → tell unsuccessful candidates promptly.
   What each role is tested on:
   - editor: software, deadlines, organising footage, colour, audio, formats, taking feedback;
   - videographer: equipment, difficult conditions, lighting, matching a director's vision,
     shoot planning, backups;
   - producer: leading a team, budgets and schedules, conflict, pre-/production/post
     workflow, vendors and talent, several projects at once.
3. **Sign a contractor agreement with confidentiality** (described, not reproduced): scope,
   dates, a fixed fee and schedule, contractor (not employee) status, confidentiality during
   and after the term, return of materials, company owns the work, 30 days' notice to end.
4. **Train** — a written process for importing, editing, colour, captions and handing in for review.

## His stated pay (`[tag: assumption] stated by S-13`)
Editors $10–20 per short reel, $100–200 per long-form edit; paid per piece as contractors.

## Outputs
A small bench of trained contractors per role.

## Sources
S-13 (hiring guide, three interview SOPs, a second producer question bank, contractor agreement).

## Open edges
- File structure, revisions and end-of-day reporting for editors are video lessons only (H-11).
- The contractor agreement has no non-solicit or IP-assignment clause beyond ownership; a
  lawyer should complete it.
