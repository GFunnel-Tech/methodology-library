# ED-05 — Niche and offer build (niche audit → positioning line → three-tier offer sheet)

`source: stated` (S-13, sections "Welcome" onboarding sheet and "Crafting Your Offer 101")
unless marked.

## Purpose
Choose a market that can pay and can be reached, say in one line what you do for it, and
turn that into a priced, three-option offer before any marketing is switched on.

## When to use
At onboarding, before ED-02 or ED-04; again whenever close rates or prices stall.

## Inputs
The owner's current offer and price, a 3-month and a 12-month goal (the onboarding sheet
asks for both), and candidate niches.

## Steps (his order)
1. **Niche audit — four yes/no tests** (S-13 · "The Niche Checklist/Audit"):
   1. They are easy to find, online or locally.
   2. You can get a meeting with the decision-maker to *diagnose*, not a quote-and-bid process.
   3. The market has money and room to grow.
   4. Your skill (content, video, strategy) solves a problem they have — stay in your lane.
   A niche must pass all of them. (His document says "all 3" but lists four; we read four.)
2. **Positioning line** — one sentence: *who* the market is, the *outcome* they get, the
   *old method* they drop, the *new method* you use. A coach approves it before moving on.
3. **Offer sheet** (S-13 · "Crafting Your Offer 101", template + worked example):
   - three packages that grow by **term and depth** (template: 3, 6, 12 months);
   - a promise (the outcome);
   - a **guarantee** that reverses the client's risk;
   - **contingencies** — what the client must do for the guarantee to hold;
   - two **incentives**: one for paying in full, one for signing the same day, each a cheap
     extra that feels valuable.
4. **Contract** — a fixed term (3/6/12 months) on a monthly fee, then month to month (see
   "Contract terms" below).

## Decision points
- A niche failing any test → choose another before building the offer.
- Guarantee only alongside contingencies that the client controls.

## Outputs
A niche, a positioning line, an offer sheet with three tiers, and an agreement.

## Contract terms (described, not reproduced; S-13 · retainer agreement and client agreement)
Both templates cover: a fixed initial term, then month to month with 30 days' written
notice; no refunds; no chargebacks (raise disputes directly); a guarantee and its
contingencies as blanks; two revisions per round; a liability cap; confidentiality; the
company may end the agreement if the client ignores its advice.
**Conflict:** the retainer template keeps content ownership with the company unless the
client buys it out; the client agreement gives the client the deliverables, with a right
for the agency to reuse footage in its own marketing. `[tag: conflict]` (H-14)

## Open edges
- "Work for free until results" is the example guarantee. It needs a written results
  definition and a time limit to be safe (H-15).
- No retainer floor or upfront percentage is stated anywhere except "first month upfront"
  in one agreement (H-8).
