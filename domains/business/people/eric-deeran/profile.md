## Who (as his own site states it — S-01, S-03)
Atlanta, GA. Founded **Deeran Films** (marketing / video agency, est. 2015; clients such as
Georgia Power and Mercedes-Benz Stadium). Spent about three years documenting his agency's
system, which became the **Inbound System**, sold through the **Master Filmmaker** program.
Audience: high-ticket service businesses, mostly videographers and creative agencies moving
from one-off projects to retainers. Self-reported scale: "828+ businesses served", "4.85/5"
`[tag: assumption] stated by S-01; unverified`.

One "hybrid" program — explicitly not a course, done-with-you or done-for-you: group cohorts
and 1-on-1 mentorship, advisory, an online training library, marketing & sales help, and a
community. No prices published; entry is "Schedule Demo" → apply.masterfilmmaker.com.
